# Generated application material never becomes Candidate evidence

The Candidate Knowledge Base only holds what the Candidate said or supplied, plus extraction-time inferences that cite it. Resumes, cover letters, Application Answers, and Strategy output stay on their Application and are never written back as Candidate Facts. If they were, the persuasive framing that Creative Latitude allows (Levels 3–4) would return as "evidence" for the next Application. Each round would inflate the Candidate a little further, and a knowledge base polluted this way cannot be cleaned afterwards. We rejected learning reusable answers from the system's own output, even though that would grow the knowledge base faster.

## Consequences

- This limits only what is *stored*, not how boldly the system writes. Creative Latitude applies in full at generation time, and no fact-by-fact validation or approval gates are introduced.
- Answers the Candidate gives in interviews, Needs You, Review edits, and profile edits are still kept automatically as Candidate Facts, with no confirmation step.
